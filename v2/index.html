01 — Why we do it

Making a difference comes first.

A lot of the work we do starts with someone who couldn't get it done on their own: an older homeowner with an overgrown yard, a small church with worn-out steps, a family with a repair that's been waiting too long.

We show up, do the work right and treat every place like it belongs to someone we know. If money is tight, say so. We'll talk it through and see what we can do.
